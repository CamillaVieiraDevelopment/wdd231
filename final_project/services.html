Our Services:

>

Psychopedagogical Assessment

The psychopedagogical assessment is a careful and comprehensive process aimed at understanding how the individual learns, identifying possible learning difficulties, strengths, and learning styles. It includes interviews, observations, playful activities, and specific assessment tools, allowing for an accurate evaluation that guides the most appropriate interventions for each case.

>

Psychopedagogical Intervention

Psychopedagogical intervention is developed based on the assessment results and aims to promote the patient’s cognitive, emotional, and academic development. Sessions are personalized and use playful, educational, and therapeutic strategies to foster autonomy, self-confidence, and enjoyment in the learning process.

>

Family Guidance

Family guidance provides support to parents and caregivers, helping them understand learning difficulties and the patient’s developmental process. Working collaboratively with the family strengthens relationships, aligns expectations, and offers practical strategies for daily life, significantly contributing to the effectiveness of psychopedagogical care.

>

Academic Support

Academic support is designed to assist students with study organization, understanding school content, and developing more effective study habits. The work respects each student’s learning pace, promoting confidence, autonomy, and improved academic performance.

>

Support for Learning Difficulties

This service is aimed at children, adolescents, and adults who experience specific learning difficulties, such as reading, writing, mathematics, attention, or memory challenges. The psychopedagogical approach seeks to identify the underlying causes of these difficulties and develop strategies to help overcome barriers in the learning process.

>

Psychopedagogical Support for Long-Term Hospitalized Patients

Psychopedagogical support in hospital settings and healthcare institutions aims to ensure continuity in the learning process while respecting the patient’s physical and emotional conditions. This service contributes to well-being, helps maintain the connection with learning, and promotes humanized care through cognitive and emotional stimulation during long-term hospitalization or treatment.
